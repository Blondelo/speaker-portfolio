I started in BD, progressed to BD Manager and later became a co-founder.

My role expanded from opening conversations and developing partnerships to taking broader responsibility for the project. I worked across community growth, external relationships, fundraising and preparation for new products.

It meant staying close to the community while also working with partners, investors and the team to move the project forward.

THE PROJECT

What we were building.

Origo was a decentralized science ecosystem on Base. Its vision brought together a launchpad for DeSci projects and crypto products designed to broaden the ecosystem.

PROJECT LAUNCHES

Launchpad

A launchpad focused on DeSci, connecting projects with a crypto community and supporting token launches and ecosystem partnerships.

PAYMENTS

Crypto card

A planned crypto card intended to connect the ecosystem with everyday spending. We were preparing for launch when the project came to an end.

STABLECOIN

OUSD

The stablecoin we were developing as part of Origo’s broader product plans. Like the card, OUSD was approaching launch when the project stopped.

BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT

Partnerships
with a purpose.

Connecting Origo with the Base and DeSci ecosystems.

We built partnerships with a wide range of projects on Base and across DeSci. I identified potential partners, opened conversations, negotiated collaborations and followed through on execution.

I also hosted weekly livestreams, X Spaces and AMAs, inviting teams from Base and DeSci to discuss their projects and connect with our community.

Our partnerships included work with DNG Worldwide, one of Malaysia’s largest pharmaceutical companies, on its token launch through Origo.

EVENTS

Bringing the
community together.

From blockchain weeks to the Health-Fi DeSci Forum.

We organized events with our community during selected blockchain weeks, bringing people together to meet the team, discuss our work and connect with other projects.

We also co-organized the Health-Fi DeSci Forum, an event with more than 1,000 attendees focused on the launch of DNG Worldwide’s token on Origo.

I presented Origo and spoke about the project, explaining our ecosystem and the collaboration with DNG Worldwide to the audience.
